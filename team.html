Meet the team

We are Syracuse University students who know how heavy a rough week can feel. Here is who built this guide.

Mari Bourne

Information Management and Technology, Cloud Computing

My name is Mari Bourne, and I am a senior at Syracuse University majoring in Information Management and Technology with a concentration in Cloud Computing. Originally from New York City, I am a social media intern for BLISTS, where I encourage Black and Latinx students to explore technology and build professional networks. I am passionate about using technology to create practical solutions for everyday challenges. I hope to start a career in the corporate beauty industry, applying consumer analytics and business analysis to help brands better understand their customers.

Katia Shevloff

Writing & Rhetoric, Law, Society & Policy

My name is Katia Shevloff, I am a senior at Syracuse University double-majoring in Writing & Rhetoric and Law, Society & Policy at the Maxwell School of Citizenship and Public Affairs. I am originally from New York City, and I’ve spent my college career building professional experience in press relations, government communications, and political rhetoric. I currently serve as Marketing Chair for Kappa Omega Alpha, a public policy professional fraternity. Looking ahead, I am passionate about pursuing a career in crisis communications or digital marketing.
